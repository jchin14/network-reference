IP ranges Wi-Fi power limits (AU)

The address blocks that hotspots, routers, Docker, VMs, VPNs and clouds use by default. Check a subnet before you set up a network, so it won’t collide with whatever someone plugs in later.

Check a subnet

Rules of thumb

  • IPv4 LAN: pick a random 10.x.y.0/24. Skip 10.0–10.10, 10.42–43, 10.64–127, 10.88–89, 10.96–111 and 10.244.
  • Avoid 172.17–172.31 if anything on the network runs Docker, WSL2 or Hyper-V.
  • 192.168.x is crowded. Anything under ~192.168.10 is a router default somewhere, plus 43, 49, 50, 56, 64–71, 86, 88, 100, 122, 137, 178.
  • Don’t use 100.64.0.0/10 for a LAN. ISPs (CGNAT) and Tailscale already live there.
  • IPv6: use fd + 40 random bits for your /48. Never use fc00::/8 or a hand-picked fd00::.

The most power (EIRP) an 802.11 device may radiate in Australia, by band and channel, under the ACMA LIPD class licence. EIRP counts antenna gain, so a higher-gain antenna means turning the radio down.

Weather radars near major cities

  • Only C-band radars (5600–5650 MHz, channels 120–128) affect 5 GHz Wi-Fi. S-band radars don’t.
  • Channels 116–128 are already banned for Wi-Fi in Australia.
  • Within ~35 km of a C-band site (further with line of sight), skip the Avoid channels. DFS can drop your AP off them without warning.
  • Safest near a radar: 36–48 or 149–165 (no DFS).
  • Same channels for every site, as the Bureau doesn’t publish per-site frequencies. 35 km is the US radius for airport weather radars.

mW ⇄ dBm

+3 dB doubles the power, +10 dB is ×10. 0 dBm = 1 mW, 20 dBm = 100 mW, 30 dBm = 1 W.

Is my setup within the limit?

  • EIRP = radio power + antenna gain − cable loss.
  • DFS: the radio must listen for radar and leave the channel when it hears it.
  • TPC: the radio can cut power by 6 dB or more. Without it, the DFS limit is halved.
  • Data: Australian 802.11 EIRP Transmit Power Limits v2.0 (Brett Verney, April 2024), from the ACMA LIPD Class Licence (Variation 2023). Section numbers refer to that licence.
  • Rules change, so check the current ACMA licence before relying on this.
